03 / DELIVERY MODEL

A clear process.
From requirement to placement.

Engagement expectations, candidate profile and turnaround are aligned with each client or partner before delivery begins.

01

Understand & qualify

Confirm role scope, must-have skills, location, work authorization, pay range, interview process and target start date.

02

Agree the engagement

Align on contract, C2H or direct-hire model; W2 or C2C route; submission format; commercial terms and points of contact.

03

Source & screen

Activate relevant talent channels, assess role fit and availability, and validate candidate interest before submission.

04

Coordinate & close

Manage interview scheduling, feedback follow-up, offer coordination and onboarding communication.

TURNAROUND APPROACH

Speed with alignment — not guesswork.

Submission timelines are set after role qualification and depend on skill scarcity, location, work authorization and market conditions. We agree on realistic milestones and keep stakeholders updated throughout.
